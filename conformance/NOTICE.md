# Upstream fixture notices

The production parser and renderer are independently implemented. Development fixtures and the import inventory contain adapted excerpts from these upstream projects. Exact commits and per-case source links are recorded in `upstreams.json` and fixture origins. Adaptations convert statically recoverable test inputs and assertions into JSON; documentation examples are catalogued separately.

- [LiquidJS, harttle and contributors](https://github.com/harttle/liquidjs): [retained MIT license](licenses/liquidjs-MIT.txt). Documentation excerpts under `docs/` are attributed to LiquidJS and provided under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); their original source links accompany inventory entries.
- [deepin-community/ruby-liquid, Shopify and contributors](https://github.com/deepin-community/ruby-liquid): [retained MIT license](licenses/ruby-liquid-MIT.txt).
- [Shopify/liquid, Shopify and contributors](https://github.com/Shopify/liquid): [retained MIT license](licenses/shopify-liquid-MIT.txt).
- [Shopify/liquid-spec, Shopify and contributors](https://github.com/Shopify/liquid-spec): [retained MIT license](licenses/liquid-spec-MIT.txt).

These notices cover the imported material; they do not establish a license for the rest of this repository.
